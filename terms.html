Terms of use

Version: 9 November 2026 · Draft for review before publication

Internal use only

Laudicom Finance Agent is intended solely for internal use by authorized personnel of participating companies. It is not offered as a public accounting service.

Authorized access

Each participating Visma.net ERP administration must separately authorize the application. Users must access only the records and administrations they are permitted to use and must protect credentials and confidential information.

Current functionality

The current Visma service integration requests read-only access for retrieval and analysis of financial data. Any future ability to create, update, delete or post accounting records requires additional authorization, controls and revised documentation.

Review of outputs

AI-generated summaries, classifications and recommendations can be inaccurate. An authorized person must verify outputs against original documents and applicable accounting requirements before relying on them for financial decisions or entries.

Availability and changes

Availability depends on third-party services, including Visma, Microsoft and Anthropic. Functionality and these terms may be updated as the internal application develops.

Contact

Questions about this application: finance@laudicom.nl. The responsible contracting/legal entity should be confirmed before publication.
