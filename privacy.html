Laudicom Finance Agent

Privacy policy

Version: 9 November 2026 · Draft for review before publication

Purpose and scope

This internal application helps authorized personnel review financial administration data for participating companies, including supplier records, invoices and related accounting information. The current Visma service integration requests read-only access.

Information processed

Depending on the records involved, information may include company details, supplier and customer contact information, names, invoice descriptions, financial amounts, tax identifiers, bank account details and email or attachment contents. Some of this information may be personal data.

Systems and service providers

The application accesses authorized Visma.net ERP administrations. Invoice-related information may also be obtained from a Microsoft 365 mailbox. When an authorized user asks Claude to analyze data, relevant content may be processed by Anthropic as the AI service provider. Accordingly, this policy does not claim that information is never shared with external service providers.

Access and safeguards

Access is intended to be limited to authorized personnel and specifically authorized company administrations. API credentials should be stored securely and must not be published in website files. The application should not perform write operations under its current read-only Visma API authorization.

Retention

Data retention depends on the underlying Visma, Microsoft 365 and AI service configurations and the participating companies' retention policies. A single universal retention period is not asserted here; the responsible organization should confirm applicable periods before publication.

Responsibility and inquiries

The participating company responsible for the relevant administration determines the purposes of its accounting data processing. For privacy questions, contact finance@laudicom.nl. The legal entity responsible for this website and the applicable privacy rights process should be confirmed before publication.
